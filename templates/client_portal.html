How It Works

1. Request: Submit a lease request from the catalog. You will receive a pending confirmation email.

2. Approval & Dispatch: Once approved, our team dispatches the microscope to your institution. You will receive a final "Lease Approved" email.

3. Return: When your lease expires, you will receive a reminder email 1 day before. You can renew directly from this portal.

Guidelines & Warnings

  • Do not attempt to disassemble the microscope or clean the internal lenses.
  • Do not use harsh chemicals or solvents on the 3D-printed parts.
  • Keep the unit away from liquids and excessive dust.
  • Always transport the microscope using the provided protective case.
  • If the stage makes a clicking noise, stop using it immediately and report it below.
